<div className="ga-steps">
  <div className="ga-step"><span className="ga-step-num">1</span><div><span className="ga-step-text">Open your project and go to any <strong>MCP Tools</strong>, then under section Quality choose <strong>Gap Analysis</strong></span></div></div>

## How to check a task

<p className="ga-text">Gap Analysis also works on the Tasks page. Here the text of the task is what is asked, and the check tells you how much of it is really in the code.</p>

<div className="ga-steps">
  <div className="ga-step"><span className="ga-step-num">1</span><div><span className="ga-step-text">Open <strong>Tasks</strong>.</span></div></div>
  <div className="ga-step"><span className="ga-step-num">2</span><div><span className="ga-step-text">Click <strong>Check gaps</strong> next to the task.</span></div></div>
  <div className="ga-step"><span className="ga-step-num">3</span><div><span className="ga-step-text">Wait about half a minute. The percentage appears next to the task.</span></div></div>
</div>

<figure className="ga-figure">
  <DocImage src="/images/gap-analysis/figure-21.png" alt="Task with the Check gaps button" />
  <figcaption className="ga-caption">A task that was never checked has the &ldquo;Check gaps&rdquo; button.</figcaption>

## From Claude Code, Codex or Cursor

<p className="ga-text">If your coding tool is connected to think4ever (MCP &amp; Tools &rarr; MCP Connect), you can do all of this without leaving the tool. Just ask it in your own words.</p>

<span className="ga-subhead">The whole concept, or a part of it:</span>

<div className="ga-prompts">
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Start a gap analysis of this project.&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Run a gap analysis of Phase 3 only.&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Analyse only the business flows and the business rules.&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Show me the results of the last gap analysis, the weakest items first.&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;What is missing in the Export My Posts flow? Show me the code.&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Compare the last two analyses.&rdquo;</div>
</div>

<span className="ga-subhead">One task:</span>

<div className="ga-prompts">
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Check the gaps of task 4312.&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Which of my tasks still have gaps?&rdquo;</div>
  <div className="ga-prompt">&ldquo;Close the gaps of this task and check it again.&rdquo;</div>
</div>

<p className="ga-text ga-text--after">The tool starts the analysis, waits for it, reads what is missing and where, and can run it again after its changes. An analysis started this way is the same one you see on your concept and on the Tasks page, with the same percentages.</p>

<div className="ga-remember"><strong>One thing to remember:</strong> the analysis reads the code that is in your think4ever project. Send your latest code to the project first.</div>
