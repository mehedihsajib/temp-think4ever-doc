# Design from Intent

This guide walks you through creating a complete application
 blueprint—from defining high-level goals to generating system
 requirements and UI concepts—using Think4ever.

![Design from intent card](/images/create-project/create-project-22.png)

## Step 1: Initiate System Design

1. From the Think4ever dashboard/home page, click on
 **Create a New Project** button.
2. Click on the **Design from Intent -** button to
 start creating your app from scratch.

## Step 2: Define the Application Objective

![Define application objective](/images/create-project/create-project-23.png)

1. On the
 **What should this application accomplish?** 
 screen, enter your main **Business objective** in
 the text area.
 

*Example: "Customers can request refunds, managers can
 review exceptions, and every decision remains visible to
 the customer."*
2. *(Optional)* Click **Attach files** or
 **Import from Jira & other tools** to provide
 context such as specs or mockups.
3. *(Alternative)* Select a pre-made template under
 **OR START FROM A READY-MADE SOLUTION DESIGN** 
 (e.g., *Creator Fan Platform* ,
 *Class Scheduling System* ).
4. Click **Continue** .

## Step 3: Answer Concept Key Questions

![Requirements Analysis Key Questions modal](/images/create-project/create-project-24.png)

A modal window titled **Key Questions** will prompt
 you to shape the initial concept:

1. Select target user roles (e.g.,
 *Writers who create & sell* ,
 *Readers who browse & buy* , or *Both* ).
2. Check allowable features and content items (e.g.,
 *Cover image* , *Tags/categories* ,
 *Pricing* ).
3. Choose platform configuration rules (e.g., platform cut
 percentage).
4. Click **Submit** .

## Step 4: Requirements Analysis & Questionnaire

![Key Questions choices](/images/create-project/create-project-25.png)

1. Think4ever will generate a
 **Requirements Analysis** summary covering:
 
**Current Requirements Overview** 
**User Roles** 
**Key Features & Business Rules** 
**Integrations**
2. Work through the follow-up clarification questions. For
 multiple-choice or edge-case questions (e.g., handling
 post-publishing updates), you can:
 
Select a specific rule option.

 Select **Let AI decide** to let the system
 determine the standard approach.
3. Click **Submit Answers** . The platform will
 process your input in real time.

![Processing your answers](/images/create-project/create-project-26.png)

## Step 5: Review & Approve Requirements

![Review and approve your requirements](/images/create-project/create-project-27.png)

1. Review the generated structured requirements under
 **Review & approve your requirements** .
2. Toggle checkboxes to include or exclude specific
 **Overview** , **Goals** , and system
 behaviors.
3. Once satisfied, click **Approve & Build** .

## Step 6: Automated Concept Build

![Building diagram from analyzed requirements](/images/create-project/create-project-28.png)

1. The platform processes all requirements and generates systemic
 diagrams, concept blocks, and logic flows automatically.
2. Wait for the processing pop-up (*"Building diagram from analyzed requirements..."* ) to finish.

## Step 7: Explore the Concept Blueprint & Next Steps

1. Upon completion, a **Concept Ready** modal will
 display a summary of generated assets (e.g.,
 *Concept blocks* , *Business flows* ,
 *Roles & permissions* , *Integrations mapping* ,
 *Data objects* , *Business rules* ).
2. Click **Start UI screens creation** or close the
 dialog.
3. You can now explore the visual canvas, review
 roles/permissions, check data objects, or toggle into
 **Dev Mode** to begin UI creation and hands-on
 coding.
