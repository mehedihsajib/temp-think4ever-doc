# Plans/Pricing
![Plans Overview](/images/portal/plans/pricing-overview-0.png)

The Plans page under the **Account** section allows you to view your current subscription tier, compare available plan options, and upgrade your account to fit your project requirements.

![Plans Overview](/images/portal/plans/plans-overview.png)

## Top Bar Overview

- **Current Plan Status Banner:** Displays your active plan tier (e.g., Think Free).

![Current Plan Status Banner](/images/portal/plans/plan-status-banner.png)

- **Manage Billing Button:** Located at the top-right corner of the main content area, allowing quick access to your billing and payment management settings.

![Manage Billing Button](/images/portal/plans/manage-billing-btn.png)

## Subscription Plans Breakdown

![Subscription Plans Breakdown](/images/portal/plans/subscription-plans-breakdown.png)

### THINK FREE (Free)

**Overview:** Designed for individual developers or beginners looking to test, build, extend, or modernize software before making any financial commitment.

**User & Workspace Capacity:** 1 User and 1 Project.

**Credits & Usage Limits:** Includes 500 total orchestration credits.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
- Build new applications, extend existing ones, or modernize legacy systems.
- Bring Your Own Model (BYOK) support.
- Easy upgrade path when you outgrow the initial free limits.

---

### THINK NEW ($25 / month)

**Overview:** Aimed at solo developers, freelancers, and startup founders who need dedicated workspace features and regular monthly resource top-ups.

**User & Workspace Capacity:** 1 User and 3 Projects.

**Credits & Usage Limits:** 2,000 orchestration credits replenished every month.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
- Dedicated company workspace.
- Connect developer tools like Claude Code, Cursor, or Codex over MCP (Model Context Protocol).
- Bring Your Own Model (BYOK) support.
- Flxible credit expansion: Add extra credits as needed at $0.05 per credit with unused purchased credits rolling over.

---

### THINK BIG ($250 / month)

**Overview:** Built for developer teams shipping software together in a shared environment with advanced collaborative and administrative controls.

**User & Workspace Capacity:** 8 Users and 20 Shared Projects in one company workspace.

**Credits & Usage Limits:** 20,000 orchestration credits per month in one shared pool.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
- Includes all capabilities from the Think New plan for the entire team.
- Add company team members with granular roles and per-project access controls.
- Centralized admin controls and usage caps.
- Parallel coding for all team members over MCP.
- Priority system orchestration for faster response times.

---

### THINK BLUE (Custom / Enterprise)

**Overview:** Tailored for larger organizations with complex environments, custom deployment requirements, and high-scale operations

**User & Workspace Capacity:** Custom number of Users, Projects, and Credits tailored to organizational needs.

**Key Features & Capabilities:**
- Enterprise-scale team management.
- Private deployment options.
- Custom usage pools and advanced cost management controls.
- Stage gates and confidence scoring for quality assurance.
- Dedicated onboarding and technical support.

## How to Upgrade Your Plan

1. Navigate to **Plans** under the **ACCOUNT** section on the left sidebar navigation menu.
2. Review the plan options and features.
3. Click the blue **Upgrade** button under the **Think New** plan card.

![Upgrade Button](/images/portal/plans/upgrade-btn.png)

4. Fill up payment details.

![Fill up payment details](/images/portal/plans/payment-details.png)

5. Once payment details are set, click on **Subscribe & Start**

![Subscribe & Start](/images/portal/plans/subscribe-and-start.png)

## Managing Billing & Invoices

To update your payment method, view invoice history, or modify auto top-up settings:

- Click the **Manage Billing** button at the top right of the Plans page, or
- Use the Billing and Auto Top-up links in the left sidebar menu.

![Managing Billing & Invoices](/images/portal/plans/billing-invoices.png)
