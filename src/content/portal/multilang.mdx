# Multi-Language Support

The Think4ever portal supports multiple languages. You can change your preferred display language at any time using the language selector dropdown located in the top-right header. Supported languages include English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Hindi, and Dutch.

## How to Change the Display Language

- Click the Language Selector icon (e.g., EN) in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
- Select your preferred language from the dropdown menu:
- The portal interface will immediately update to your chosen language.
