# Multi-Language Support

The Think4ever portal supports multiple languages. You can change your preferred display language at any time using the language selector dropdown located in the top-right header. Supported languages include English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Hindi, Dutch, and Arabic.

## How to Change the Display Language

1. Click the Language Selector icon (e.g., **EN**) in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
2. Select your preferred language from the dropdown menu:
   - **EN** – English
   - **ES** – Español (Spanish)
   - **PT** – Português (Portuguese)
   - **FR** – Français (French)
   - **DE** – Deutsch (German)
   - **HI** – हिन्दी (Hindi)
   - **NL** – Nederlands (Dutch)
   - **AR** – Arabic
3. The portal interface will immediately update to your chosen language.
