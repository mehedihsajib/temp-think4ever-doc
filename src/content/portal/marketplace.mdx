# Marketplace

## What is the Marketplace?

The Marketplace is the part of think4ever where you hire talent or find work. Clients post jobs, review proposals and bring a freelancer into their project. Freelancers build a profile, browse open jobs and send proposals. One account can do both.

## What you can do

**As a client you can:**
- Post jobs and save drafts.
- Search and filter freelancers by skills, rate, country, availability and rating.
- Review proposals and manage contracts.
- Message freelancers in a shared timeline.

**As a freelancer you can:**
- Build a marketplace profile so clients can find you.
- Browse open jobs and filter them by budget and experience level.
- Send proposals with your rate and timeline, and answer screening questions.
- Track proposals, active jobs and contracts.

### Set up your marketplace profile

Open the **My Profile** tab to describe yourself. A completeness bar at the top shows how much of your profile is filled in.

- Under "I am using the marketplace as", choose Client, Freelancer or Both.
- Enter a Headline (at least 10 characters), for example your main role and skills.
- Write your About section (at least 50 characters is required to be listed; 200 or more fills the completeness bar).
- Add your Skills. Pick from the library or type your own and press Enter.
- Choose a main Category and up to 3 specialties.
- Optionally add an Hourly rate in USD. It is for information only and can be left empty.
- Set your Availability to Available, Busy or Unavailable.
- Add your Portfolio or website and your Country, then save.

</Callout>

## PART 2: Working as a client

### Review your Overview

In Client view, the Overview tab shows five counters:

- **Open jobs:** jobs currently accepting proposals.
- **Drafts:** jobs you saved but have not posted.
- **Proposals received:** proposals sent to your jobs.
- **Offers pending:** offers waiting for a freelancer to respond.
- **Active contracts:** contracts that are currently running.

Below the counters, the Hire talent card has a Post a job button, and the Your marketplace profile card has an Edit profile button.

### Find freelancers

1. Click the Find Freelancers tab.
2. Type a name, headline or bio into the search box, or use the filters on the left.
3. Use the sort menu (Best match) to change the order of results.
4. Click View profile on a result card to see the full profile.
5. Click Save search to keep your current search and filters.

![Figure 5: Find Freelancers with filters and a result card](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-5.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 5: Find Freelancers with filters and a result card</p>

**Available filters:**
- Category and Skills (comma-separated).
- Hourly rate (USD) with minimum and maximum.
- Country.
- Availability: Any, Available or Busy.
- Rating: Any, 4.5+, 4.0+ or 3.0+.
- Talent badge: Any, Top Rated or Rising.

Click Clear filters to reset everything. Each result card shows the freelancer's name, badges such as Email verified, availability, headline, skills, hourly rate, rating, completed contracts, location and a short bio.

### Manage your jobs

The Jobs tab lists every job you posted under My jobs.

- Search your jobs with the search box.
- Filter by category, budget type (Any budget, Fixed or Hourly) and experience level (Any level, Entry, Intermediate or Expert).
- Sort by Newest first.
- Each job shows its status, budget, posting date, number of proposals and how many freelancers are hired, for example 1 of 1 hired.

![Figure 6: My jobs list with search and filters](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-6.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 6: My jobs list with search and filters</p>

### Post a job

1. Open the Jobs tab and click Post a job. You can also click Post a job on the Overview tab.
2. Enter a Title, for example "Build a booking page for my photography studio".
3. Describe the work, your expectations and anything a freelancer needs to know.
4. Choose a Category.
5. Select the Experience level: Any level, Entry, Intermediate or Expert.
6. Set the Project duration: Not sure yet, Under 1 month, 1-3 months, 3-6 months or 6+ months.
7. Set the Hours per week: Not sure yet, Less than 30 hrs/week or More than 30 hrs/week.
8. Choose the Budget type, Fixed price or Hourly, and enter the Budget amount in USD.
9. Enter the Skills you need, separated by commas. At least one is required.
10. Set the Number of freelancers needed. The job stays open until this many contracts are accepted, or until you close
11. Optional: tick "This is a contract-to-hire opportunity" if the role could become a full-time position.

![Figure 7: Post a job form](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-7.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 7: Post a job form</p>

### Add screening questions and attach files

- Under Screening questions (optional, up to 5), type your own question or pick a suggestion, then click Add. Freelancers must answer these questions when they send a proposal.
- Click Attach files to add supporting documents.
- Click Post job to publish, Save as draft to finish later, or Cancel to close the form.

![Figure 8: Bottom of the form with screening questions and the Post job button](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-8.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 8: Bottom of the form with screening questions and the Post job button</p>

### Connect a Think project (optional)

The panel on the right side of the form is called Think brain. It lets you link the job to a Think project so Think can help specify the requirements with you. Click Create a new project to start a fresh project, or Connect an existing project to pick one you already have.


<Callout type="note" title="Note">
Linking a project only gives Think context for the posting. Nobody gets access to your project until you accept a freelancer and confirm the contract.
</Callout>

### Track your contracts

The Contracts tab lists contracts under As client. Each row shows the job title, the freelancer, the assigned workspace, the status (for example active or ended) and whether workspace access is active or removed.

![Figure 9: Contracts tab in Client view](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-9.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 9: Contracts tab in Client view</p>

<Callout type="note" title="Note">
Reviews and completed-contract counts come from contracts completed on think4ever. Payment is arranged outside the platform and is not verified by think4ever.

### Send and read messages

1. Click the Messages tab.
2. Select a conversation from the list on the left.
3. Type in the message box at the bottom and click the blue send button. Use the paperclip icon to attach a file.

![Figure 10: Messages with conversation list and message box](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-10.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 10: Messages with conversation list and message box</p>

Marketplace events such as offers, contracts and access changes also appear in the same conversation, so everything about a job stays in one place.

## PART 3: Working as a freelancer

### Review your Overview

Click Freelancer view at the top right. The Overview tab shows five counters: Invitations, Proposals pending, Offers to review, Active contracts and Contracts completed. The Find work card has a Browse jobs button.

<Callout type="tip" title="Tip">
If your profile is hidden, the Your marketplace profile card shows an orange warning. Click Edit profile to complete it so clients can find you.
</Callout>

### Browse jobs

1. In Freelancer view, click the Jobs tab, or click Browse jobs on the Overview.
2. Search by keyword in the Search jobs box.
3. Filter by category, budget type (Any budget, Fixed or Hourly) and experience level (Any level, Entry, Intermediate or Expert).
4. Sort by Newest first, or click Save search to keep your filters.

![Figure 11: Browse jobs in Freelancer view](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-11.png)

<p className="text-center text-sm text-slate-500 dark:text-neutral-400 mt-1.5 mb-6">Figure 11: Browse jobs in Freelancer view</p>

Each job card shows the title, budget, client, posting date, duration, hours per week, an Open status and the required skills.

### Send a proposal

1. Click a job card to open its details.
2. Read the description, scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria and required skills.
3. Scroll down to Send a proposal and introduce yourself. Explain why you are a great fit.
4. Enter Your rate (USD) and your Timeline, for example 2-3 weeks.

### Answer the screening questions

If the client added screening questions, they appear below the proposal. Every answer is required.

1. Read each question carefully and type your answer in the field below it.
2. Check your rate, timeline and answers.
3. Click Send proposal at the bottom of the window.

![Figure 14: Screening questions with the Send proposal button](/images/portal/marketplace/figure-14.png#400)

You can follow the status of your proposals under My Proposals and see accepted work under Active Jobs.
</Callout>

## Best practices

- Complete your profile first. A hidden profile cannot be found in Find Freelancers.
- Write clear job titles and descriptions, and list the skills you need.
- Use screening questions to quickly find the best candidates.
- Set a realistic budget, duration and hours per week.
- Keep your availability up to date.
- Use Messages to keep all job conversations in one place.

## Final remarks

The Marketplace connects people who need work done with people who can do it, without leaving think4ever. Complete your profile, post clear jobs or send thoughtful proposals, and keep your conversations and contracts in one place.

**Hire smart. Work smart. Build together.**
