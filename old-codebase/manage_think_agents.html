Navigating "Think Agents"

The Think Agents menu provides a centralized hub to manage and deploy specialized AI agents tailored to specific business domains, regulatory frameworks, and operational tasks.

Core Management

  • Manage Think Agents: Click this option to configure, customize, or overview your active AI agents.
  • Custom Think Agents: A dedicated section displaying custom-built agents tailored specifically to your organization's unique workflows (Note: dynamically shows "No custom agents yet" if none have been created).

Global Think Agents (Pre-Built Categories)

The platform features an extensive library of out-of-the-box, domain-specific AI agents grouped into the following specialized categories:

Category Menu Description
Architecture & Standards Review, validate, and align project structures against corporate, technical, or industry-standard blueprints.
Finance & Accounting Assist with financial modeling, bookkeeping logic, auditing checks, and general financial analysis tasks.
Security & AppSec Analyze system architecture, configurations, or inputs to identify potential vulnerabilities and ensure strict application security compliance.
Engineering & Code Quality Evaluate code repositories, logic flow, and development practices to maintain high technical standards and minimize technical debt.
Data & Analytics Process data sets, generate insights, and assist with data modeling, pipelining, and analytics validation.
Legal, Compliance & Risk Audit documents and workflows against legal standards, regulatory frameworks, and risk mitigation policies.
HR & People Streamline talent acquisition workflows, organizational structure planning, and policy documentation.
Sales & CRM Optimize customer relationship management strategies, sales pipelines, and client communication frameworks.
Marketing & Content Assist with campaign planning, target audience analysis, brand alignment, and content generation standards.
Customer Support Enhance customer service operations by modeling ticketing logic, FAQ responses, and support workflows.
Operations & Corporate Streamline day-to-day organizational workflows, internal logistics, and corporate administration tasks.
Healthcare Specialized tools configured to handle domain-specific healthcare administrative requirements and medical operational workflows.
Retail & E-commerce Optimize digital storefront workflows, inventory management logic, and retail consumer experience strategies.
Manufacturing & Supply Chain Address logistics, procurement workflows, production line logic, and supply chain tracking standards.
Real Estate & Property Evaluate property data, contract logic, market analysis templates, and real estate asset management.
IT & DevOps Automate and validate cloud infrastructure setups, deployment pipelines, and system operations workflows.
