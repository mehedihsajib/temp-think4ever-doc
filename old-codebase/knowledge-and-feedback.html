Knowledge and Feedback

The Knowledge & Feedback section is located at the bottom of the left-hand sidebar. This suite of tools is designed to handle "soft" project data—documentation, team sentiment, and cross-functional communication—ensuring that information is not just stored, but acted upon.

Pages (The Knowledge Base)

As detailed previously, Pages is your project’s internal Wikipedia. It uses a block-based editor to house:

  • Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs): Guidelines for how the team should handle specific technical tasks.
  • Project Wikis: Centralized technical specs, brand guidelines, and onboarding materials.
  • Shared Assets: A repository for images, files, and templates used across the project.

Feedback

The Feedback module is used to gather input from stakeholders, beta testers, or internal team members

  • Centralized Input: Instead of scattered emails or chat messages, feedback is logged here as specific entries.
  • Actionable Insights: Feedback can be triaged and converted directly into Issues (Bugs or Features) if the input requires a technical change.
  • Sentiment Tracking: Helps project managers gauge the "health" of a feature based on user and team reactions.

Collaboration

The Collaboration module is the "Live" component of your documentation and feedback loop. It focuses on real-time teamwork:

  • Shared Workspaces: See which team members are currently active in specific Pages or Issues.
  • Comments & Mentions: Use @mentions to alert specific users to feedback entries or documentation updates that require their attention. >
  • Version History: Track changes made to the Knowledge Base to ensure everyone is working from the most recent "source of truth."
