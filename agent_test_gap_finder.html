Test Gap Finder

The Test Gap Finder agent maps your test coverage onto critical business flows to identify blind spots in your validation suite.

Core Mechanics

  • Business Flow Alignment: Maps existing test suites directly to core operational and business logic flows rather than raw lines of code.
  • Dual-Scope Selection: Targets specific business flows and corresponding test files concurrently, pre-selecting all assets by default for an exhaustive evaluation.
  • Cross-Reference Analysis: Parses each designated business flow against existing test cases to isolate unvalidated paths, conditions, or missing assertions.
  • Gap Telemetry & Recommendations: Generates a targeted breakdown of coverage percentages per business flow alongside automated recommendations for missing test scripts.

Step by Step Guide

Step 1: Select Flows and Test Suites

Review your project workspace to select the specific business flows and test files you want to audit. Keep all items checked by default to execute a comprehensive, platform-wide gap analysis.

Step 2: Verify the Analysis Plan

Examine the preliminary blueprint generated by the agent. This step outlines how your test scripts will be mapped against your business logic models. Click confirm to authorize the tracking sequence.

Step 3: Run the Verification Engine

Allow the agent to process your files. The engine traces every operational node within your business flows, cross-referencing them with your existing test assertions to identify functional holes.

Step 4: Review Flow Coverage and Deploy Suggested Tests

Open the final telemetry report to evaluate your testing health:

  • Coverage per Flow: Review the explicit coverage percentage assigned to each critical business path to prioritize your testing efforts.
  • Suggested Tests: Use the automatically generated test case blueprints to write missing scripts, closing critical validation gaps before deployment.
